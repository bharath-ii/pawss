Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated on September 25, 2026

PAWWS is committed to fair, transparent, and customer-first refunds. This policy governs all bookings made through the PAWWS platform.

Cancellation Window

When You CancelRefund Amount
More than 4 hours before the booking100% full refund
Within 4 hours of the booking start timeUp to 70% (30% provider compensation fee may apply)
Service provider no-show100% immediate refund

How to Cancel

You can cancel a booking directly from the app:

  • Go to Bookings > Select your active booking > Tap Cancel Booking.
  • Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
  • For disputes or assistance, contact support@pawws.app or WhatsApp +91 98765 43210.

Non-Refundable Situations

  • Bookings cancelled after the service has commenced.
  • Cancellations due to incorrect pet information provided by the customer.
  • Cases where the customer was unreachable at the service address at the scheduled time.

Platform Credits

In certain cases, PAWWS may issue platform credits (PAWWS Coins) in lieu of a cash refund as a goodwill gesture. These credits can be used for future bookings on the platform and have a validity of 12 months from the date of issuance.

Payment Gateway Timelines

Refunds to UPI and debit cards may take 3–5 business days. Credit card refunds may take 7–10 business days depending on your bank's processing cycle. PAWWS is not liable for delays caused by third-party payment processors.
