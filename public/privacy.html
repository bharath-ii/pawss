Privacy Policy

Last updated on September 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes the policies and procedures on the collection, use, disclosure, and protection of your information when you use the PAWWS mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, "Platform/s") made available by PAWWS Pet Marketplace Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (collectively referred to as "PAWWS", "we", "us", and "our").

The terms "you" and "your" refer to the user of the Platforms. The term "Services" refers to any services offered by PAWWS whether on the Platforms or otherwise.

Please read this Policy before using the Platforms or submitting any personal information to PAWWS. This Policy is a part of and incorporated within, and is to be read along with, the Terms of Service.

Your Consent

By using the Platforms and the Services, you agree and consent to the collection, transfer, use, storage, disclosure, and sharing of your information as described and collected by us in accordance with this Policy. If you do not agree with the Policy, please do not use or access the Platform.

Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide pet care marketplace services, including:

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, and profile picture.
  • Pet Profiles: Pet name, species, breed, age, weight, vaccination records, dietary requirements, and medical history.
  • Location Data: Precise or approximate GPS location when finding nearby service providers or enabling live tracking for home-delivered pet services.
  • Payment Data: Transaction details, billing address, and payment confirmation IDs. Note: Full card numbers and UPI MPINs are securely handled by our PCI-DSS certified payment gateway and are never stored on PAWWS servers.
  • Communications: Messages exchanged between pet owners and service providers via in-app chat.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is utilized strictly for service delivery:

  • Connecting pet parents with verified pet care professionals (groomers, vets, walkers, trainers, boarding facilities).
  • Calculating real-time travel distances and service availability using Google Maps.
  • Processing bookings, refunds, platform loyalty credits, and invoices.
  • Delivering critical service updates, appointment reminders, and emergency alerts.
  • Preventing fraud, verifying partner credentials, and ensuring animal safety standards.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Assigned Service Providers: Partner receives customer contact name, pet profile, and service address solely to fulfill the booked service.
  • Google Maps Platform: Location coordinates for distance calculation and map rendering.
  • Payment Gateways: Encrypted transaction identifiers for payment settlement.
  • Cloud Providers: Encrypted cloud database and push notification delivery (FCM).

Data Retention & Your Rights

You maintain complete control over your personal data:

  • Access & Correction: You may view and edit your profile, pets, and address details at any time in the Account section.
  • Data Portability: You may request an export of your booking and pet medical records by contacting us.
  • Account Deletion: You may permanently delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app under Settings > Data & Privacy > Delete Account. Upon deletion, your profile, pet records, saved addresses, and active credentials will be permanently erased within 30 days, subject only to statutory tax record retention requirements.

Children's Privacy

PAWWS services are not directed to individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

Google Maps Attribution

Map data and location features in this application are powered by Google Maps Platform. Use of Google Maps is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms of Service.
